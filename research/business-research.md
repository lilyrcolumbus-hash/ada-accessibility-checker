## TOP 3 FINAL IDEAS (Validated)

### IDEA #1: ADA Website Accessibility Checker (SELECTED)
- **What:** Web app that scans websites for WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- **Why NOW:** April 26, 2026 deadline (DOJ rule makes WCAG 2.1 AA the official standard)
- **Market:** 98% of websites don't comply. 4,000+ lawsuits/year. 67% target small businesses
- **Penalty:** Up to $150,000 per violation. Typical settlement $5,000-$20,000
- **Competitors gap:** accessiBe ($49-349/mo, fined $1M by FTC for false claims), WAVE (free but limited, no monitoring), enterprise tools ($500+/mo). NOBODY serves small businesses at $29/mo with monitoring
- **Revenue model:** Free scan (1 page) → $29-99/mo monitoring + alerts + reports
- **Stack ($0):** Netlify (free) + Supabase (free) + Stripe. No AI API needed - pure HTML analysis
- **SEO strategy:** Programmatic pages: "ADA compliance check [industry] [state]" = thousands of pages
- **Key data:** Serial lawsuit filers (Brian Whitaker 1,700+ lawsuits, Orlando Garcia 800+) scan websites automatically. We do the same but PROTECT businesses instead

### IDEA #2: State-by-State AI Compliance Tracker
- **What:** Web app where businesses select state + industry → see all AI regulations + deadlines + alerts
- **Why NOW:** Colorado AI Act (already in effect), Texas TRAIGA (Jan 2026), California CCPA AI (Jan 2027)
- **Competitors:** ZERO. Confirmed by G2/Capterra analysis. No tool exists for state-by-state AI compliance
- **Penalty:** $2,500-$7,500 per violation (California)
- **Revenue model:** Free (1 regulation) → $19-49/mo all alerts
- **Stack ($0):** Same as above. Data + logic, no AI API needed
- **SEO:** 50 states × 10 industries = 500+ programmatic pages
- **Market:** Gartner predicts AI Governance market = $492M in 2026, $1B+ by 2030

### IDEA #3: COI Tracker for Small Contractors
- **What:** Portal for general contractors to track subcontractor insurance certificates
- **Why:** 70% of COIs are non-compliant. 250,000+ small construction companies in USA
- **Competitors gap:** All use "contact sales" pricing. No solution under $300/mo for small contractors
- **Revenue model:** $49-99/mo
- **Stack ($0):** Same. Manual entry V1, add OCR later with revenue
- **Construction has 1.4% AI adoption** - lowest of all industries
